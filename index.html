Robert Antwen Medical Researcher

Robert Antwen, a medical researcher with over 26 years' experience, is dedicated to studying metabolic disorders and uncovering natural solutions for type 2 diabetes. He focuses on restoring the body's natural glucose-regulating enzymes rather than relying on conventional drugs. A collaborator with researchers from Harvard, Yale, and Boston University, his work centers on science-backed herbal medicine and metabolic health. Outside the lab, he enjoys family time, hiking, healthy cooking, and teaching others how to live longer, medication-free lives.
